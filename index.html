Hi, I'm Katie!
Meet your reading coach

A decade in the classroom. One big plot twist.

I spent ten years teaching first grade in the inner city. Then I trained as a certified dyslexia specialist, and it changed everything about how I teach reading.

Now I use structured, multisensory, science-of-reading instruction to help kids decode with confidence. Every child deserves to feel the joy of reading, and every parent deserves a clear plan to get them there.

Can't wait to connect!
– Katie

  • 🎓Certified dyslexia specialist, trained in structured literacy
  • 🍎10 years teaching first grade
  • 🧠Science-of-reading, multisensory instruction
  • 🏫Trainings for schools, libraries & homeschool families
