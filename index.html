Hey there! I'm Katie, and I am fueled by a burning passion to make a profound impact on young lives through the power of literacy.

With years of experience as an educator and a heart full of dedication, my mission is crystal clear: to unlock the magic of reading and empower young minds to reach new heights!

Whether it's one-on-one tutoring or offering valuable consulting to parents and teachers, I am committed to equipping students with the essential tools to conquer the world of words.
